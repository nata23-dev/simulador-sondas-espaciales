                      Spacecraft Ephemeris File Release

Spacecraft Ephemeris: SAT286

Location: ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/eph/spacecraft/vgr1.SAT286.nio

Timespan from JED  2444458.50(07-AUG-1980) to JED  2444563.50(20-NOV-1980)
Equinox Reference Julian Date: 2451545.00
Planetary Ephemeris Number: DE-0421/LE-0421

Release to: Horizons/NAIF                                Time: 05-May-2008
Preparer: R. A. Jacobson

Comments:
Smoothed Voyager 1 trajectory for the Saturn encounter consistent with the
SAT286 planetary ephemeris and the SAT286 satellite ephemeris.  This file
was created from a fit to the Voyager Doppler, range, satellite optical, and
ring radio occultation data.  This trajectory is an update to that described
in the reference.

Reference: Reconstruction of the Voyager Saturn Encounter Orbits in the ICRF
System, R.A.Jacobson (JPL/Caltech) AAS Paper~03-198 presented at the AAS/AIAA
Space Flight Mechanics Meeting, Ponce, Puerto Rico, February 2003




On May 5, 2008 Bob Jacbson provided two new Voyager spacecraft trajectory files for the
Saturn flybys: one for Voyager 1 and one for Voyager 2.  The files are named vgr1.sat286.bsp
and vgr2.sat286.bsp respectively.

Both files are consistent with the recently released SAT286 Saturn satellite ephemeris, and
with the DE421 planetary ephemeris.

According to Jacobson these two Voyager solutions are still not the absolute final ones for
Saturn--he expects to have another set completed around July 2008 at the conclusion of
Cassini nominal mission.  (However, Jacobson indicates that further changes to the Voyager
spacecrat position rrelative to Saturn and Saturn's satellites will be quite small.)
