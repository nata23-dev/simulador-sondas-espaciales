Smoothed Voyager 1 trajectory for the Saturn encounter consistent with the
SAT261 ephemerides and gravity field.  This is the file used in the processing
of the Voyager 1 radiometric and optical data for the SAT261 global solution.



The Voyager 1 Saturn reconstructed spacecraft trajectory file:

  vg1.jup.sat261.bsp

was produced by R. Jacobson of the  JPL Solar System Dynamics Group
as part of his work in improving the ephemerides of Saturn's
natural satellites. It was made using Voyager spacecraft observations
during the Saturn flyby, and also using Cassini observations of
Saturn's satellites.

NAIF has no "accuracy" information about the SPICE SPK file made
from the source file identified above.  R. Jacobson says new Voyager 1
trajectory solutions are still being frequently produced, based on new
Cassini data, but that the changes between solutions are much smaller
than the solution uncertainties.  Jacbson has no plans for making any
kind of significantly different (better) solution.
