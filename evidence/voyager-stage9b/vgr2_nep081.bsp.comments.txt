                      Spacecraft Ephemeris File Release

Spacecraft Ephemeris: NEP081

Location: ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/eph/spacecraft/vgr2.nep081.nio

Timespan from JED  2447478.50(13-NOV-1988) to JED  2447801.00(01-OCT-1989)
Equinox Reference Julian Date: 2451545.00
Planetary Ephemeris Number: DE-0421/LE-0421

Release to: Horizons/NAIF                      Time: 08-Apr-2009 10:00:00
Preparer: R. A. Jacobson

Comments:
Smoothed Voyager 2 trajectory for the Neptune encounter consistent with the
NEP081 planetary ephemeris and the NEP081 satellite ephemeris.  This file
was created from a fit to the Voyager Doppler, range, VLBI, and satellite
occultation data.  The planetary ephemeris was fit to the Earthbased astrometric
data, the planet occultations, and the Voyager data. The satellite ephemerides
were fit to the Earthbased astrometric data and the Voyager data.  This
solution is an update to the NEP076 solution to be consistent with the NEP081
satellite ephemeris and dynamical constants.

References:
1. R. A. Jacobson, Reconstruction of the Voyager~2 Neptune Encounter in the
   ICRF System, AIAA Paper 2008 7372 presented at the AIAA/AAS Astrodynamics
   Specialist Conference, Honolulu, HI, August 2008; American Institute of
   Aeronautics and Astronautics, Reston, VA (NEP076)
2. R. A. Jacobson, The orbits of the Neptunian satellites and the orientation
   of the pole of Neptune, AJ, 137, 4322-4329, 2009 (NEP081)
