COMMENTS =

[The Voyager 2 "supertrajectory" nio file was obtained from
George Lewis by Chuck Acton on 23 May 1996; below are Lewis'
comments.]

"The file voy2_st.ftp is the Voyager 2 supertrajectory (i.e. a
P file in ftp format) with a corrected Neptune encounter. The SEPV
target parameters were the Neptune periapsis time and the Triton
B-plane coordinates. The Neptune B-plane coordinates are
both within 10 km of the achieved coordinates. The Triton
periapsis time is within two seconds of the achieved periapsis
time.

"The M04513 satellite ephemeris was substituted
for the NEP004 satellite ephemeris. The planetary ephemeris
DE118 was retained. The NEP004 ephemeris should be used with
the 1987 version of the Voyager 2 supertrajectory.

"A fictitious instantaneous delta-v of about 4 m/s
was used at 1 Oct 89 00:00:00 ET to correct the outgoing
asymptote. The SEPV target parameters were the position
vector components at 1 Jan 2031 as given by the M05016U
trajectory.

GDL
1 Jun 95

"N.B. "Supertrajectory" was used as a handy label to denote
the approximate Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 trajectories that
were constructed beginning at Earth departure and extending
to 2050. The files provide approximate trajectory information
throughout the Voyager mission. The last maintenance effort
was made by S. E. Matousek in 1987 as described in IOM
Voy-NAV-87-16, dated 31 Mar 87. "

END_COMMENTS
